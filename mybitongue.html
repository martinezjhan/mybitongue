Natural Disasters: Be Prepared!

Natural disasters can happen in many places. Earthquakes, hurricanes, tornadoes, and volcanic eruptions can damage homes, roads, and buildings. During an earthquake, the ground shakes because continental plates move. Sometimes, buildings are demolished because they aren't safe. However, earthquake-resistant buildings are designed to protect people.

Before a disaster, emergency plans are prepared by families and communities. Safe shelters are chosen, and important supplies are stored. Enough clean water, food, a flashlight, and a first-aid kit should be kept in a safe place. These things are very important when electricity and water are not available.

During a hurricane, strong winds can break windows and trees. Too much rain can cause floods, and too many dangerous objects can become flying debris. People should stay calm and follow safety instructions. If a volcanic eruption happens, hot lava can flow from the crater. People must move away from dangerous areas when authorities tell them to leave.

After a disaster, damaged buildings are checked by experts. Food and water are shared with families who need help. Recovery takes time, but communities can become stronger when people work together. Being prepared is important because everyone deserves a safe place to live.
